Welcome to Digital Agriculture Lab

Digital Agriculture Lab at the University of Wisconsin–Madison investigates agricultural and environmental systems across scales using remote sensing and machine learning. Our research bridges satellite, drone, and field-scale observations to monitor crop and environmental conditions from individual fields to regional and broader landscapes. By integrating multi-scale Earth observations with advances in machine learning and artificial intelligence, we develop methods to characterize spatial and temporal variability, extract actionable information, and support data-driven decision-making in agriculture and the environment.
