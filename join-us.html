PhD Opportunities

Our lab is recruiting one PhD student at the University of Wisconsin–Madison starting from Fall 2027. Candidates with research experience in machine learning/deep learning, remote sensing, and precision agriculture are highly desired. Ideal candidates would have an M.S. degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Agricultural/Biological Engineering, Civil Engineering, Geography, or other related disciplines. Candidates with a research focus on one of the following two areas are strongly encouraged to apply: 1) Develop machine learning models with satellite remote sensing data for precision agriculture; 2) Precision agriculture using UAV multi-sensor data (e.g., hyperspectral, RGB, LiDAR).

Minimum English requirement: TOEFL 80 for tests taken before January 21, 2026; 4.5 for tests taken on and after January 21, 2026; or IELTS 6.5.

Application Process: Interested candidates can email their CV and a cover letter to Dr. Zhou Zhang (zzhang347@wisc.edu). Qualified candidates will receive email feedback immediately.
